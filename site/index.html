Decide, Don't Generate

Companion widgets

Four small tools that let you move the numbers in the book yourself. They run entirely in your browser. All data is synthetic: Kestrel Logistics is fictional, and every Jev probability comes from jev-mock-synthetic, not the real model.

Threshold simulator

Set what mistakes cost, and watch the act, review and escalate thresholds move over Kestrel's live week. Chapter 14.

Calibration playground

Fix a model's probabilities with temperature or Platt scaling, with as many or as few labels as you like. Chapters 3 and 11.

Bake-off explorer

Weight the scorecard's columns and see which of six methods comes out ahead. Chapter 13.

Decision cost calculator

Your volumes, your prices: what decisions cost, and which become worth making. Chapters 9, 12 and 16.
