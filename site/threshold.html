Threshold simulator

Synthetic: Kestrel's live week, jev-mock-synthetic probabilities after Platt scaling

Set what mistakes cost at Kestrel. The thresholds come from the Chapter 14 formulas; the counts come from running those thresholds over the 5,027 alerts of the live week.

Formulas: low = review ÷ (miss × (1 − reviewer miss rate)); high = (page − review) ÷ (page + reviewer miss rate × miss). These assume the probabilities are calibrated, which is why the book tests them first (Chapter 11).
